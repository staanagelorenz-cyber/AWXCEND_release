# AWXCEND 1.4.27 (build 43)

Meet your buddy.

- Your AI buddy can now chat with you. Give it a name in AI Buddy, ask about training, food or the app, and it remembers your goals. Up to 20 messages a day.
- Your chats stay on this phone unless you choose to keep them on our server. You can change this in Settings.
- A new orb shows your buddy and glows with its mood. It waits in a corner of every screen; tap it to chat, or turn it off in buddy settings.
- Your buddy can speak. Download its voice once (about 95 MB), pick a female or male voice, or use your phone's own voice.
- On Android, during a workout your buddy can stay beside you over other apps (off until you turn it on).
- AI Buddy, plan tips and food scan now run on Cloudflare, so they work any time. The privacy notice is updated to say so.
