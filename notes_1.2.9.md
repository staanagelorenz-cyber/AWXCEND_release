## AWXCEND 1.2.9

- **Help inside every screen.** The first time you open Food, Log Workout, Camera Workout, Trailblaze or Plans, a short guide walks you through it with examples of what to type. Tap the **?** at the top of any of them to see it again.
- **Dungeon alerts.** Your phone now tells you when a Dungeon gate opens. Turn it off in Settings → Notifications → Dungeon gates opening.
- Log Meal: the Unit box is wider, so "serving" is no longer cut off.

Install: download **AWXCEND.apk** below and open it. Updating keeps your account and data.
