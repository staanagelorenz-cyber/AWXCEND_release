# AWXCEND 1.4.25 (build 41)

A safer, fairer community.

- Your post, your comments: on your own post, open a comment’s menu and tap Remove from my post.
- Moderators can now remove a reported post, comment or chat message. If something of yours is removed, you are told why, and it can be restored if it was a mistake.
- Every moderator action now needs a written reason and is kept in a log that can’t be edited.
- Staff roles are stricter: one Owner, then admins, then moderators. Changing roles and permanent bans ask staff for their password again.
