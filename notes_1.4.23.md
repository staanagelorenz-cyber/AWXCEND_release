# AWXCEND 1.4.23 (build 39)

The System on your home screen, and backup trackers.

- New home-screen widgets in four sizes: a slim System bar, a Coach tile, Today and the Status window. Hold your home screen, tap Widgets and find AWXCEND.
- The widget follows your day: your meal at meal times, your session and first lift on training days, Trailblaze at your usual walk or run time, and a live view with your rest timer while you train.
- Tap ↻ on any widget to fix it to Meals, Training or Trailblaze; each widget keeps its own choice. Calories and macros show only if you turn them on in Settings → Reminders.
- Backup trackers for 10 camera exercises: if the main tracker keeps missing your reps, the app offers a backup that compares you with a pre-built reference. Exercises with a backup have a small green dot.
- Log Meal: typing from a label? Enter its serving size and how much you had, and the app multiplies it for you (for example a 1.2 L bottle labelled per 300 ml).
- Streak reminders at times you choose (Settings → Reminders), and a gentle nudge if you have been away for a few days.
- Change your password in Settings → Account. Clear messages when something goes wrong, instead of error codes, and nothing waits forever.
- Drafts are saved as you type, actions show at once, and small fixes everywhere. From now on, small fixes reach the app without a new download.
- Updated Privacy Notice and Terms: please read and accept the short list of changes when asked.
