# Changelog

## 1.2.3 (build 6) — 2026-10-15

- Run / Walk / Ride: the no-signal message fades after a few seconds; tap "No signal" to read it again.

## 1.2.2 (build 5) — 2026-10-15

- Meal plan and Pantry: Add another food, then save them all at once.
- Pantry foods can have a photo, seen by whoever you share your pantry with.

## 1.2.1 (build 4) — 2026-10-15

- When rest is over the phone buzzes and plays the rest sound twice at full volume.
- Camera workout: one "How to do it" button (3D movement and camera setup together).

## 1.2.0 (build 3) — 2026-10-15

- A simpler home screen: four places (Train, Food, The Veil, Ranking) instead of ten tiles.
- Train: camera workout, Log Workout, Plans (your month and your routines), Run / Walk / Ride and the Recovery map.
- Food: Log now, Meal plan, Pantry and Budget in one place; a shopping list priced with Food Budget.
- Body & progress (body fat, weight, waist) in the Body tab of your profile; sign-up can take your body fat.
- Routines remember last time and suggest when to add weight; a weekly recap every Sunday.
- The app shows its version and tells you when a newer one is out.

## 1.1 (build 2)

- First release of the AWXCEND app.
