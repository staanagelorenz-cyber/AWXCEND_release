# Changelog

## 1.4.27 (build 43) — 2026-11-28

- Your AI buddy can now chat with you. Give it a name in AI Buddy, ask about training, food or the app, and it remembers your goals. Up to 20 messages a day.
- Your chats stay on this phone unless you choose to keep them on our server. You can change this in Settings.
- A new orb shows your buddy and glows with its mood. It waits in a corner of every screen; tap it to chat, or turn it off in buddy settings.
- Your buddy can speak. Download its voice once (about 95 MB), pick a female or male voice, or use your phone's own voice.
- On Android, during a workout your buddy can stay beside you over other apps (off until you turn it on).
- AI Buddy, plan tips and food scan now run on Cloudflare, so they work any time. The privacy notice is updated to say so.

## 1.4.26 (build 42) — 2026-11-18

- A new look when AWXCEND opens: the X logo while it loads, and a short intro the first time on a new phone (tap to skip).
- Tap the logo at the top of Home to switch to the X logo. Your choice is remembered.
- Rank up is now a full cinematic, and Quest cleared counts up the XP you earned.
- New moments: NEW RECORD when you beat your best, and streak celebrations at 7, 30 and 100 days.
- The launch screen now shows the X logo.
- Fixed: finishing a big routine no longer stops with "Slow down", friend requests and the shop now say what went wrong, and a meditation that did not save can be saved again.
- Safer and fairer: limits on camera XP, one report per thing reported, and stronger moderator checks.

## 1.4.25 (build 41) — 2026-11-17

- Your post, your comments: on your own post, open a comment’s menu and tap Remove from my post.
- Moderators can now remove a reported post, comment or chat message. If something of yours is removed, you are told why, and it can be restored if it was a mistake.
- Every moderator action now needs a written reason and is kept in a log that can’t be edited.
- Staff roles are stricter: one Owner, then admins, then moderators. Changing roles and permanent bans ask staff for their password again.

## 1.4.24 (build 40) — 2026-11-16

- Widgets fit every home screen: on a shorter widget, the least important lines step aside instead of being cut off.
- To switch a widget, tap its circling arrows; on the Coach tile, tap the round badge in the corner. Each widget keeps its own choice.
- Fixed: switching to a backup tracker in the middle of a set now keeps the reps already counted.
- The Verified player notice is now listed in Profile → Privacy, so you can read it again any time.
- Cleaner look: small symbols across the app are now proper icons.

## 1.4.23 (build 39) — 2026-11-15

- New home-screen widgets in four sizes: a slim System bar, a Coach tile, Today and the Status window. Hold your home screen, tap Widgets and find AWXCEND.
- The widget follows your day: your meal at meal times, your session and first lift on training days, Trailblaze at your usual walk or run time, and a live view with your rest timer while you train.
- Tap ↻ on any widget to fix it to Meals, Training or Trailblaze; each widget keeps its own choice. Calories and macros show only if you turn them on in Settings → Reminders.
- Backup trackers for 10 camera exercises: if the main tracker keeps missing your reps, the app offers a backup that compares you with a pre-built reference. Exercises with a backup have a small green dot.
- Log Meal: typing from a label? Enter its serving size and how much you had, and the app multiplies it for you (for example a 1.2 L bottle labelled per 300 ml).
- Streak reminders at times you choose (Settings → Reminders), and a gentle nudge if you have been away for a few days.
- Change your password in Settings → Account. Clear messages when something goes wrong, instead of error codes, and nothing waits forever.
- Drafts are saved as you type, actions show at once, and small fixes everywhere. From now on, small fixes reach the app without a new download.
- Updated Privacy Notice and Terms: please read and accept the short list of changes when asked.
