# Changelog

## 1.4.13 (build 29) — 2026-10-30

- AWXCEND now speaks Tagalog: pick it on the login screen or in Settings → Language. Every screen, the app tour, the guides and the error help are translated.
- Fixed: weights filled in from your last session could land on the wrong set. Each set now gets its own.
- Route image: choose the stats it shows (for example steps only, or distance, calories and climb).
- Download my data now makes a readable PDF: a summary page, sections by topic, tables for workouts and meals, plain dates.
- Easier with screen readers and voice control on the home screen.

## 1.4.12 (build 28) — 2026-10-30

- Auto finish line fixed: real walking routes along streets, paths and parks, any distance from 0.3 to 15 km, with the route drawn on the map.
- Trailblaze: Suggest a route (loop or out-and-back, any length).
- Run or train together with a nearby player or a friend: same start, your own pace, no winner.
- Routines and monthly plans fill in your last weights and tell you when to go heavier.
- A weight over 500 kg names the exact exercise and set, with a one-tap fix.
- Typo-tolerant search for foods, exercises, pantry and players.
- The 1v1 camera panel folds into a slim bar.

## 1.4.11 (build 27) — 2026-10-24

- PvP in Quests: find a rival at your level, or invite a friend to a friendly match (camera reps, longest hold, GPS race).
- Domain challenges (18+): tap a nearby player to send a friend request or a duel, including a race to a finish line on the map. Names are shared only if they accept.
- Rest timer: live countdown in the notification shade and an alarm-style end sound.
- Updated privacy notice and terms.

## 1.4.10 (build 26) — 2026-10-24

- While running a routine or your monthly plan, a book button under the sound button shows how to do the current exercise (pictures, steps, muscles) without stopping your workout.
- Library updates (React, the 3D body viewer, map and sign-in plugins).

## 1.4.9 (build 25) — 2026-10-23

- Progress photos in Body & progress: daily, weekly or monthly reminders; compare, timeline, same-pose guide and time-lapse. Photos stay on your phone (and Downloads/AWXCEND/Progress), never uploaded.
- 98 Filipino foods: menudo, afritada, ampalaya with egg, sinigang, kare-kare, silog plates, siomai, halo-halo, taho and more.
- Download my data saves a readable PDF and the full data file to Downloads/AWXCEND.
- Louder rest timer alert when the app is closed.
- Unsend a routine, macro or plan card you sent in a chat.

## 1.4.8 (build 24) — 2026-10-23

- Your rank emblem now sits in the middle of its frame.

## 1.4.7 (build 23) — 2026-10-23

- Gymunnity: tap React to react, hold it for all reactions. Save, Edit, Report and Delete are in the ⋯ menu, and comments fit small phones.
- Domain: your phone vibrates and plays a sound when a player comes near, even with the app closed or the screen off.
- Your rank emblem sits in a frame in your rank's colour.

## 1.4.6 (build 22) — 2026-10-23

- Players under 18 ask a parent or guardian to confirm by email before they can chat, message, post or add friends. Training, meals and plans work right away.
- The Privacy Notice and Terms now name the operator and the Data Protection Officer, explain your rights wherever you live, and say we will keep adding features.
- Downloading your data from Settings works again.

## 1.4.5 (build 21) — 2026-10-23

- Reply to a comment on a Gymunnity post. Your reply quotes theirs, and you can react to or report any comment.
- Your rank emblem is now your picture on the dashboard and profile.
- Every screen has a guide, including Gymunnity, Ranking, Profile, Settings, Recovery and Body, and they explain small features like the Heatmap and Domain. Tap ? to see one again.
- Tab names no longer run into each other on small phones.

## 1.4.4 (build 20) — 2026-10-23

- Every rank now has its own emblem, from Bronze to the top tier, with a crest for your training path. You see it when you rank up, on your dashboard and on your profile.
- Reaching level 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 shows a special milestone frame.

## 1.4.3 (build 19) — 2026-10-23

- The 2D recovery map now shows the same detailed anatomical body as the 3D one, front and back.
- The strength leaderboard ranks 54 lifts, up from 18: leg press, leg extension, cable rows, face pulls, lateral raises, pec deck, triceps pushdowns and many more, grouped by body part.

## 1.4.2 (build 18) — 2026-10-23

- Trailblaze measures climb and descent with the phone's air-pressure sensor when it has one: much steadier than GPS height, and it works with no signal. Phones without the sensor keep using GPS height.
- Shared route images carry two AWXCEND marks instead of three.

## 1.4.1 (build 17) — 2026-10-22

- Smaller app: 92 MB to 58.5 MB, same look.
- Lite mode: slower phones switch to a lighter look by themselves so everything stays smooth (Settings > Performance: Auto, Full or Lite).
- Dashboard and exercise pictures load from your phone first: instant, and they work with no signal.
- Ready to go heavier: the same weight 3 sessions in a row with your reps holding? Log Workout suggests +2.5 kg, and Body lists every lift that is ready.
- Recovery map: switch between 3D and a simple 2D front-and-back view.

## 1.4.0 (build 16) — 2026-10-22

- Monthly plan from your routines: choose which routine goes on which day (for example Leg day on Monday); it repeats every week.
- In a chat with a friend, tap + to ask for, or send, a routine, macro targets or a monthly plan. They add it with one tap.
- Friends → Coaching: find a coach, or switch on "I coach". Verified coaches had their certificates reviewed; community helpers are friends who are not verified. Players under 18 can only be coached by a verified coach with a guardian's OK. No payments in AWXCEND.
- You get a notice when someone asks you to coach or accepts; a tag in the chat shows who is coaching whom; moderators and admins have a Coaching tab with an audit trail.

## 1.3.1 (build 15) — 2026-10-22

- Friends: "People you can add" list, so you can find players without searching a name.

## 1.3.0 (build 14) — 2026-10-22

- Planned rest days keep your streak (up to two in a row); optional cardio or meditation on a rest day.
- New Meditation (Train → Meditation): timed session, Do Not Disturb, leaving the app over 3 s ends it; 30 XP-earning minutes a day.
- Trailblaze keeps recording while you use other screens ("Back to your run").
- Trailblaze counts real steps with the phone's step sensor, checked against distance; standing still adds no GPS distance.
- Rest timer alerts you with its sound even with the app closed or the screen off.
- Fairer XP: impossible speeds and more than 6 hours of timed training a day are refused.
