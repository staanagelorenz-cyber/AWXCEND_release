# AWXCEND 1.4.26 (build 42)

The System awakens.

- A new look when AWXCEND opens: the X logo while it loads, and a short intro the first time on a new phone (tap to skip).
- Tap the logo at the top of Home to switch to the X logo. Your choice is remembered.
- Rank up is now a full cinematic, and Quest cleared counts up the XP you earned.
- New moments: NEW RECORD when you beat your best, and streak celebrations at 7, 30 and 100 days.
- The launch screen now shows the X logo.
- Fixed: finishing a big routine no longer stops with "Slow down", friend requests and the shop now say what went wrong, and a meditation that did not save can be saved again.
- Safer and fairer: limits on camera XP, one report per thing reported, and stronger moderator checks.
