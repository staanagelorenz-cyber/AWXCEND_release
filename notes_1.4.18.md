AWXCEND 1.4.18 (build 34)

- Food posts in Gymunnity now have Add to meal plan: pick the day, the meal and how much. The nutrition and ingredients come with it, and your plan keeps its own copy.
- Planned meals can have ingredients. Add or edit them under any meal in your plan, and the shopping list shows them for each dish.
- Food Budget: compare the cost of any amount of protein, not just 25 g. Pick 10 to 50 g or type your own; your phone remembers it.
- Save your route image in Portrait or Landscape (choose it under What the image shows).
- Admins and moderators now have a small tag beside their name, so you know who runs and looks after the community.
